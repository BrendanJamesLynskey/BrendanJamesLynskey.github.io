Profile

Engineer with over 25 years' experience across hardware, FPGA, SoC, DSP and software. Much of it designing and verifying FPGA or SoC systems (packet switching, high-speed serial links, cable modem PHY/MAC, CPU debug, broadcast video, cryptography), then board-level hardware for AI accelerators at Blaize: power delivery, signal integrity and DDR. For the last two years almost entirely software, mainly Python, building agentic AI systems with LangGraph and MCP; Claude Code every day. Keen to work where a hardware background counts, such as new compute architectures.
